The balanced panel of 25 Indian banks observed over 19 years (N = 25, T = 19; 475 observations) to investigate the dynamic relationship between bank net interest income (NII) and a small set of explanatory variables: bank size (log total assets), GDP growth and inflation. Panel stationarity checks were performed(ADF per entity), test for cointegration using an Engle–Granger style residual ADF from a pooled model with entity/time dummies, estimate a within (fixed effects) model, check cross-sectional dependence, and run per-bank Granger causality tests. Main findings: (i) most bank-level series are non-stationary at levels (ADF by entity); (ii) residuals from a pooled regression with entity and time dummies are stationary (strong evidence of cointegration among the included variables in the pooled sense); (iii) fixed-effects (within) estimation on demeaned data finds positive and statistically significant associations of log total assets, GDP growth and inflation with NII; (iv) there is strong cross-sectional dependence across banks, signalling common shocks; (v) per-bank Granger tests show more cases where GDP growth helps predict NII than vice versa. Robustness checks done(panel cointegration, FMOLS/DOLS, factor-augmented methods).
